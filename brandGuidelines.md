# Brand Guidelines: TEFA Javanica (Roastery Division - Light Industrial)

## Brand Persona
Clean, Precise, Professional, and Industrial.

## Visual Theme: Light Industrial
- **Concept**: Laboratory-clean with industrial accents. 
- **Background**: Light Gray / Off-White to keep it fresh and readable.

## Color Palette (HEX)
- **Primary**:#4C2700 (Dark Espresso - for main text and headers)
- [cite_start]**Primary Action**: #C87A54 (Earthy Terracotta - for main buttons and brand identity)
- **Secondary**: #526B42 (Leafy Green - for success states/stock safe indicators)
- **Accent**: #B3885B (Natural Wicker - for subtle highlights or small icons)
- **Background**: #F5F5F4 (Stone White - Very light gray-beige)
- **Surface/Cards**: #FFFFFF (Pure White) with soft borders.

## UI Elements
- **Borders**: Thin, defined lines (##B3885B) to give a "blueprint" feel.
- **Shadows**: Very subtle (soft) to keep the cards looking flat and modern.
- [cite_start]**Components**: High-contrast buttons using the Earthy Terracotta for clear action hierarchy.
